## Planning and review

When a genuine uncertainty remains, offer short natural Indonesian alternatives with the
same facts, numbers and qualifications. Explain whether the difference concerns warmth,
directness, register, emphasis or vocabulary. Allow existing wording and author own wording.
Use an Indonesian writer/evaluator isolated from English draft prose. Do not manufacture a
choice for every paragraph or ask again where a contextual preference is settled.

Keep native-language evaluation. The evaluator flags real errors separately from choices
among acceptable words. Preserve an acceptable author-approved expression. When he chooses
an exception to a style-only rule, record its narrow context and update affected guidance.
Do not allow a preference to alter technical meaning, evidence or decision-changing caveats.
