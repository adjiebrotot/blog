# Prompt for an image model

For when Adjie generates the picture himself. Fill in the brackets from the brief in
`SKILL.md`, and keep the style block as it is so every picture on the blog matches.

```text
A [man / woman / older man / young woman ...] with [skin tone, hair], wearing [a plain
orange t-shirt ...], [doing the action: holds a calculator in both hands and checks the
total], in [setting: at a wooden desk with a receipt and a piggy bank]. Mid-shot, head to
waist, 4:3.

Style: clean flat digital illustration in the style of a how-to guide step picture.
Realistic, simplified proportions. Every shape has a thin outline in a darker shade of its
own colour, never black. Flat, fairly saturated fills with one hard-edged soft shadow
tone (cel shading), light from the upper right. Simple friendly face: small almond eyes
with a heavy upper lid line, a one-stroke nose, a small closed smile. Pale, quiet,
light blue-grey background with a soft gradient and almost no detail. No text, no letters,
no logo, no watermark, no signature.
```

Variants:

- **Close-up:** replace "Mid-shot, head to waist" with "Close-up on the hands and the
  [object], a bit of sleeve visible".
- **Comparison:** "Split diagonally into two panels by a thick dark line: on the left
  [...], on the right [...], each with its own pale background colour."
- **Back view:** "Seen from behind and slightly to the side, so we see what they are
  looking at: [...]".

Don't name wikiHow in the prompt. Some models refuse a brand name, and the look is fully
described without it. Check the result against the checklist in `SKILL.md`; image models
like to add text and extra fingers.
