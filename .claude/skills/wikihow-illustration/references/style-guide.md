# Style guide: the wikiHow look

What makes a picture read as "wikiHow style", taken from the step pictures Adjie shared
(a man reading a book in a chair, a woman holding up two shirts, a painter at an easel with
a split-panel palette close-up, a man painting a beach scene from behind). Use it to word
a prompt and to judge what comes back.

## The feel

- **Friendly and plain.** Ordinary people in ordinary clothes, in a calm good mood. A small
  closed smile or a concentrating look. Never a grin, never a pose for the camera.
- **Realistic proportions, simplified.** Not chibi, not big-eyed, not a caricature. Detail
  is left out, not exaggerated.
- **One action.** Everything in the frame serves the verb of the step. The object in the
  hands is drawn clearly, and a little larger than life if that helps it read.

## Line

- Every shape has a thin, even outline in a **darker shade of its own colour**, never
  black: skin gets a warm brown, an orange shirt a burnt orange.
- Few inner lines: a nose is one stroke, a hand one or two finger lines, a fold one curve.

## Colour and shading

- Clean, fairly saturated fills for the person and the main object (orange, teal, green,
  red, pink, mid blue shirts; warm skin; brown, ginger, black or blond hair).
- Soft shading: a darker tone on the side away from the light, gentle highlights on hair
  and glossy things. Painterly at the edges, never glossy, 3D or photo-real.
- The background is the palest, least saturated part: light blue-grey, pale green, pale sky,
  with a soft gradient, a big soft blob, or a hint of a wall, window or horizon.

## Faces and hands

- Small almond eyes with a heavier upper lid line, eyebrows as one soft stroke, a one-stroke
  nose, a small curved mouth. Looking down at the object is often truer to the step than
  looking at the reader.
- Hands hold things the way people do: a book by its edges with the thumbs on the pages, a
  phone in the palm with the thumb on the screen, a hanger by its hook. Check the fingers.

## Composition

- **4:3**, like wikiHow.
- **Mid-shot** by default: head in the upper third, hands and object at the centre, the body
  cut by the frame or a table edge.
- **Close-up** for a detail step, **split panel** (a thick diagonal line, a different
  background each side) for a comparison, **back view** when what they look at matters more.
- Props in the foreground may overlap the body (a desk edge, a plant, a mug). Few of them.

## What to reject

- Black or thick comic outlines, or no outlines at all.
- Gloss, glow, 3D rendering, photo-realism, heavy texture.
- Busy backgrounds, patterned walls, many props.
- Anime eyes, exaggerated cartoon proportions.
- Detailed or garbled text (notes, receipts, lists), a logo, a watermark or a signature.
  Short, correct, readable text is allowed.
