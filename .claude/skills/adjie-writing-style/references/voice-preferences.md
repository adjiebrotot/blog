learning system. It learns only when the workflow saves explicit author decisions here.

## How to collect a preference

During planning, offer short alternative passages only for genuinely doubtful voice or
word choices. Name the context and trade-off; allow retain current wording or own wording.
Keep claims, numbers and qualifications identical across alternatives. Do not make a
factual correction a tone option. A draft may legitimately have no doubtful passages.

## How to record and use it

Record only an explicit selection, correction or approval. Silence, defaults, evaluator
rewrites, and an unreviewed generated post are not preferences. Do not fabricate example
approvals. After an explicit choice, save its exact wording and scope before final delivery.
For Indonesian also maintain the language-specific reference and the existing correction
list when appropriate; do not impose an Indonesian choice on English.

Apply approved choices in similar contexts, not as global substitutions. One selection
is contextual evidence, not a universal rule. Generalise only after repeated consistent
choices or explicit author instruction. Where approvals conflict, retain both contexts;
ask for clarification if the same context conflicts. Explicit newer instruction wins.
Never replace an acceptable plain word merely to sound livelier. Preserve numbers,
technical meaning and material conditions through every style edit.
