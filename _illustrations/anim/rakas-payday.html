How it was made

1 · generate

Four sheets and a plate

Nano Banana 2.1, with the blog's hybrid examples attached: a neutral pose, the same man taken apart into eleven pieces, nine faces, hands and props, and an empty park with nobody in it.

2 · rig

A cut-out puppet

The pieces are lifted off white by flood fill, given a pivot at each joint and chained into a bone tree. Faces and hands are swapped, never redrawn.

  • Pieces11 body, 9 faces, 3 extra hands
  • Jointships, 2 legs, waist, neck, 2×(shoulder, elbow, wrist)
  • Perspectivefeet on the path, eye height 1.6 m
  • Shadowhis own silhouette, laid on the paving
3 · animate

One function of time

Every frame is computed from the clock alone: a walk cycle driven by distance walked, eased keyframes for the acting, timed swaps for blinks. That is what lets the timeline scrub backwards as cleanly as it plays.
