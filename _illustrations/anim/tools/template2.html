How it was made

1 · generate

Atlases, not one drawing

Nano Banana 2.1 with the blog's hybrid examples attached: Raka turned three-quarters, his body in nine pieces with whole arms and legs, twelve heads (expressions plus a looking-down angle), nine hands, and the empty park.

2 · rig

Bending meshes on bones

Each arm and leg is one drawing on a grid of triangles, weighted across two or three bones, so elbows and knees bend instead of hinging. The hair and the shirt hem have bones of their own that trail behind.

  • Bones19, with hair and hem
  • Atlases12 + 5 heads, 9 hands
  • Legstwo-bone IK, feet planted on the path
  • RendererWebGL, skinned on the CPU
3 · animate

Principles over sine waves

Footsteps are planted on the photographed path and the hips ride over them, so nothing slides. Every big move has an anticipation and an overshoot, the head and hair follow a beat late, and the turn to camera happens on a blink.
